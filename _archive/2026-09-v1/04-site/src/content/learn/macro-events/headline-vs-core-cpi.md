## What happened

Friday 11 September 2026. CPI released at 8:30 AM ET, 6:00 PM IST.

Headline CPI came in at 0.4%. That matched what was expected exactly. Nothing moved.

Core CPI came in at 0.3%. Expected was 0.2%. Gold turned down within two minutes. NQ followed.
The Dollar picked up a bid.

Same release. Two numbers. Only one of them moved price.

## What headline CPI is

Headline CPI measures the full basket of prices. Food and energy are in it. Those two move
around a lot, for reasons that have nothing to do with the economy's underlying trend.

## What core CPI is

Core CPI strips food and energy out. What's left moves slower. Traders and the Fed both watch
this number more closely, because it tells you what's actually changing underneath.

## Why headline usually doesn't move price

Most of the time, the market has already worked out where headline CPI will land. Oil prices
and food prices are visible in real time all month. By release day, headline is mostly priced
in already.

## Why core is the one that surprises

Core is harder to predict. It depends on services, wages, and rent, numbers that don't show up
anywhere until release day. That's why core is where the real surprise usually sits.

## How to trade around this

I don't react to headline unless it's a big miss. I wait for core. If core matches expected,
I expect a flat reaction. If core misses by 0.1% or more in either direction, I expect a real
move, and I already have a level marked before the number drops.

## My experience, not official

I could not tell you which way this breaks before it breaks. My read going into most CPI days
is close to 50/50 on direction. What I do know is which number to watch, and that's most of
the edge.
