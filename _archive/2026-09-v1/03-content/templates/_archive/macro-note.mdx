Open with the scene. Where you were, what you were watching, what happened first.
This is the "7:30 PM, two screens" beat — ground the reader in the moment before

## What became clear afterward

The hindsight view, dated and labeled as hindsight. *"I am telling you that on
Saturday, with the candle finished on my screen. I could not have told you that
at 8 PM on Friday."* This section only works if the previous section was honest
about not knowing yet.
