I was watching the calendar all week for this one.

Friday NFP came in at 162K. The street was looking for 55K. That is a big beat.

The market read it fast. Rate hike odds for September moved from 49% to 72% in the same
morning. That move is the setup for today.

CPI drops at 8:30 AM ET. 6:00 PM IST. Two numbers matter here. Headline CPI, and core CPI.

Headline is expected at 0.4%. That is already priced in. It will not move price much on its
own.

Core is the one I am watching. It is expected at 0.2%, same as last month. If it holds there,
I don't expect much. If it comes in hot, hike odds go up further. Gold and NQ would likely drop.
The Dollar would likely rise.
