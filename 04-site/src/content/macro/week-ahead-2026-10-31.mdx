## What happened this week

Three days. Three hits.

Wednesday, the Fed held rates at 3.75 to 4%. But the vote was 10 to 2. Two members wanted a hike right then. I wrote about that night in my journal.

Thursday, the data came in hot. The economy grew 3.4% last quarter. Headline PCE rose 0.5% in one month. That is 3.6% for the year. Core PCE was 0.3%, as expected.

Friday, a tanker was hit in the Strait of Hormuz. Oil went up. Gold fell to a one year low. Nasdaq fell hard.

Oil up. Everything else down.

## What is priced in now

On Wednesday, about 82% expected a December hike. Now it is about 71%.

That surprised me. Hot data, and still the rate hike probability came down. When stocks fell on Friday, money ran into bonds. Yields came down with it.

So the market still expects a hike. Just a little less sure than on Wednesday.

## Nasdaq: it left the box

On Wednesday I said I was stepping away from Nasdaq. Price was chopping inside a small box. I said I would come back when it closed outside.

Friday, it closed outside. Below the box.

<ShotFrame src={shot02} mobile={phone02} alt="NQ daily: Friday closed below the area of small consolidation, next liquidity at 27,493.75" caption="NQ daily: Friday closed below the box" id="shot-02" />

Now I am bearish. I am not looking for longs.

The next liquidity I am watching is 27,493.75. That is the August low in the current contract.

What changes my mind. The easy one is this. Price takes back the small consolidation box, roughly 29,100 to 30,100. Or the daily candles start closing bullish. Until then, I stay bearish.

## Gold: the floor broke

My rule for gold is simple. I am not looking for bullish until the daily or weekly chart shows bullish. Right now, neither does.

In my last post, I was watching the liquidity near 3,955. Friday took it. Gold closed near 3,884.

## Oil and the dollar

### Oil: still climbing

Oil is the only one going up. From the October low to the recent high, about 20.7%.

The next round level is 106.75. That is the September high. I want to see if it gets there.

<ShotFrame src={shot06} mobile={phone06} alt="Oil daily: up about 20.7% from the October low, next liquidity at 106.75" caption="Oil daily: next round liquidity at 106.75" id="shot-06" />

Remember the chain. Oil up, prices up, rate hike fear up. That pressure lands on gold and Nasdaq.

### Dollar: going nowhere

The dollar is not bullish. Not bearish either. It is moving sideways inside a range, roughly 101.5 to 102.9.

I treat it as a range until it shows real momentum. A range does not tell me much.

## What could change the story

The one thing that can flip gold is peace.

On Saturday, MRKT showed a new headline. Iranian envoys arrived in Doha for talks with Qatari mediators. The aim is ending the US and Iran conflict.

<ShotFrame src={shot08} mobile={phone08} alt="MRKT: what could flip gold bullish, Iranian envoys arrive in Doha for talks" caption="MRKT: the headlines that could flip gold" id="shot-08" />

If talks work, oil falls. Then rate hike fear fades. That takes pressure off gold. Talks are not a deal though. I will wait for price to show it.
