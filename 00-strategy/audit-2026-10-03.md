## What passes

- **Contrast:** every text pair I tested passes AA except rose on the black surfaces (4 pairs, item 1). Direction colours, white cards, muted text and the wine band all pass.
- **Touch and layout:** no horizontal scroll at 320, 375 or 1280px. All buttons are 44px except the items above. Text is 17px on phone (#67), 72 characters per line on the reading page (#73), line height 1.7 (#72).
- **Performance (production build):** about 14 KB of CSS, 2 KB of inline JavaScript, no framework JavaScript. Images are WebP with width and height set (no layout jump, #19), lazy loaded (#47). Fonts are self-hosted with `font-display: swap` (#50).
- **Semantics:** one h1 per page, header, main, footer and labelled navs present, native `<dialog>` for the proof and screenshots, `aria-pressed` on the picker, `aria-expanded` on tips and menu. Sticky header does not hide focused items (`scroll-padding-top`, #100).
- **Motion:** number flip and the room-left meter respect reduced motion (#9, #99). No auto-playing or looping animation (#12).
- **Colour is never the only signal:** every direction has an arrow and a word (#37).
- **Brand voice:** 0 em dashes, 0 spaced hyphens, one sentence over 16 words ("Small 5K and 10K accounts, so you can trade Nasdaq as a CFD without much at stake."). No raw hex outside tokens except the `theme-color` tag.
- **Affiliate rules:** `/go/fundednext` and `/go/mrkt` are 302 redirects with `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`. Apex is a direct link with `rel="noopener"` and "Not an affiliate link". Code shows disclosure and verified date. Canonical URLs match the sitemap.

## Where the skill disagrees with our brand (no change recommended)

Its design-system query ("dark editorial trading and macro blog, wine and rose accent") suggested a light Swiss Modernism theme, pink `#EC4899` accent, Libre Bodoni with Public Sans, and a scroll storytelling page pattern. Our dark wine brand, Aptos or Figtree headings and Source Serif body text are deliberate, so I treated these as noise. Its rules on contrast, focus, reduced motion and touch targets are the useful part, and they produced items 1 to 4 and 7 to 10 above.

Reading page size (18px, 760px) differs from brand-schema (21px, 900px) by your decision on 3 Oct. The skill's own rules support the smaller size (#67, #72, #73).
