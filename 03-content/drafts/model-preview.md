## Piece 3. /how-i-trade, "My model, a preview"

**Top**
All my payouts came from 20 to 30 minutes a day.
Not hours of charting. Here is how my day runs.

**Widget A. My day (tap each step)**
Top strip, three times, big and simple (New York time first, India time under it):
- 9:30 AM · 7:00 PM IST · The market opens. I start watching.
- 9:40 AM · 7:10 PM IST · I look again.
- 9:45 AM · 7:15 PM IST · I start trading.

Steps:
1. **Before the open.** I look at the daily first. I want good ranges. If the daily is going nowhere, I stay out.
2. **1 hour.** This gives me direction. Which way is price leaning?
3. **5 minute.** Is it moving with range, or chopping? If it is chopping, I wait for it to break.
4. **The 15 minute wait.** The first 15 minutes are wild. Many moves are false and stops get hit fast. I let it cool, then I join.
5. **1 minute.** (locked) My entry model lives here.
6. **One trade.** One trade a day. Win or loss, same rule.
7. **Lock the account.** Tradovate lets me lock it. I do. Then I go and do something else.

Text between the two widgets: Two checks decide if I trade at all. Try them.

**Widget B. Would I trade today?**
- Daily: Good ranges / Choppy
- 5 minute: Displacing / Chopping
- Result, daily choppy: **No trade today.** The daily is not going anywhere.
- Result, 5 minute chopping: **Wait.** I do nothing until the 5 minute range breaks.
- Result, both good: **Go to the 1 minute.** That is where my entry model lives.
