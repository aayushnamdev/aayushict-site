# The Twenties Are Not for Growth. They Are for Survival.

## I used to think I did not have time

I always used to think that I don't have time. That I need to do it quickly, because that is what the twenties are for. Get success fast. Understand that this is what you are doing. Make something, make money, make yourself proud. Or what? I don't know.

I don't use the word proud anymore. Earlier I used to think I will take two payouts out of these prop firms and I will feel good. Right now I don't. Because the old habits are not gone. They are still there, still ready to creep out whenever there is a single crack inside the wall.

## What the mind says on a lost day

My mind is stuck somewhere else now. I just want good profitability. Whatever I am putting in as an expense, whatever I have already put in, I want to go beyond that and leave it behind as a small mark.

I am not close to that. That is the challenge.

That is what the mind says on a lost day. You are not in surplus. You are still in the same town from where you started.

And it is a convincing thing to say. It also neglects the growth that happened in the last three months.

## Kharar to Kolkata

In terms of life, a lot of things changed. I was sitting in a small town in Kharar, in Mohali. Now I am in Kolkata.

Six months back, eight months back, I used to think about a job. Now I am a corporate guy. I joined, I am spending my time on technical writing, and on creation, on Instagram and all that. Getting a good response. People saying thank you bro for putting this information out. They are loving it.

All of that happened. And still, on a lost day, the mind reports back that nothing moved.

## Not for growth. For survival.

What I can see now is that the twenties are not for growth. They are for survival. They are for adversity.

I know what my adversity is. I know it when it knocks on the door. And I know the automatic response, which is that I just open it up.

That is what I don't like. There is a constant pressure of being perfect. If you can recognize it, then why can't you change it? Why are you not able to change it till now?

## The default mode

That is a very long process. I can understand it from the perspective of psychology also. I know all the answers, and still there is something which stops, which just goes back to the default mode.

That default mode is learned over years. From childhood till now, through the twenties, through twenty five. It is tough, because your mind does not put you in easy survival mode for a very long time. Even if a change happens, it makes you feel bad.

## Some days I use melatonin

Some days I use melatonin. I know I am under the limit, but I need to use it. Because obviously, you can win the world but you cannot win your mind. Some days that is exactly what it is. I use it to decrease the brain activity, to make it flow and grow.

There is growth. There is a thing there. There is betterment if I put myself in the plot. Yes, it is an upward graph, but with very deep retracements, from the perspective of trading. I know every side of what I do. That is what it is.

I am happy that I am able to have these opportunities in my life, and that all these adversities are things I can see, I can feel, with all the emotions and all the intellectuality.

Some days it is too much. It is too much from the perspective of what the current capabilities can handle.

I don't know what to rely on. Not the addictive substances, because I know they are going to destroy more. The temptations are there, but it is not helping, so I just don't do that. Coping can be learned. The process of learning it is very, very difficult.

## The loop

Do I read a lot? No. Do I journal a lot? No. I don't know.

I have this thinking pattern, this constant, where whatever I make, whatever I did, loops. It is a constant loop.

Today I was able to pin down what I had regretted and what I did not. So I guess the twenties are difficult. I don't think it was ever meant to be easy.

If these peak years were easy, they would be wasted. I am seeing other people who have easy access. They are ruining it. Because the human mind needs something. It needs something to struggle on.

## The king who wanted a war

That is why in the whole of history we had wars.

A king was sitting well with his kingdom. Everything was nice. But now he wants struggle, because that is unconscious, that is already coming from inside. That is why he went for the wars, where the mass destruction happened, and where even he also suffered. He can name it glory. He can name it anything he wants.

There were many battles. And in them, even the strongest lost.

The human mind, the unconscious mind, wants you to suffer, and wants to keep the suffering.

## He is not your friend

You need to understand that he is not your friend. At times, do not treat him like one. Do not give him space in your house.

Be aware. Because awareness is the first level of inside awareness. Long awareness will make you introspect automatically.

That is what it is. And that is what is going to make you change.
