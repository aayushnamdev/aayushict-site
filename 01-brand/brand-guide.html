03 Fonts

Four fonts, four jobs

Each font does one job only. Never swap them around.

Headings
Aptos SemiBold

Every title and heading. Weight 600. Aptos is Microsoft's font, so phones without Office show Figtree instead, which looks very close.

All body text. Chosen for calm, long reading. Free from Google Fonts.

Nav, buttons, toggles, captions, meta lines. Nothing longer than a line.

06 Voice

How the words sound

The look is calm. The words should be too.

  • First person. I read, I traded, I don't remember.
  • Sentences of about 9 to 13 words.
  • Plain Indian English. Explain jargon on first use with a term tip.
  • No em dashes, no hyphens joining thoughts. Use full stops or commas.
  • Never "hawkish" or "dovish". Say what the Fed is likely to do.
  • Real numbers only. If something isn't remembered, say so.
  • Show losing trades too. They stay visible.
  • Every market post ends with: Not advice. Stop loss on.
