# Voice guide

Applies to every page, every label, every piece of copy on the site. Source: build-plan-v2.md §2
plus measured rules from Aayush's own drafts.

## Hard rules
- **Average sentence length 9-13 words.** Measured across his drafts: 9.4-10.4 words average.
  Shorter than every benchmark checked (Maggiulli 14.3, Carlson 16.6, Housel 19.8,
  vedictrades.blog 27.7). LLM default prose runs 18-25 words. This is the single biggest thing
  that makes his writing structurally impossible to mistake for AI.
- **Never em dashes or hyphens as sentence connectors.** Use commas, periods, or restructure.
- No added italics, pull quotes, or blockquotes for style. No emoji in headings.
- Never smooth or tighten the spoken grammar. Keep it exactly as drafted.
- Keep honest uncertainty in the text where it was real ("I could not tell you", "my read was
  50/50"). Do not resolve it into false hindsight confidence.
- Never change a number, ticker, price level, or date from source material.
- First person, like the reader is sitting next to you ("I watch...", "Here is what happens...").
- Every number carries a consequence, not just a value.
- Never "hawkish" or "dovish". Say "rate hike probability", "priced in", or describe the mechanism.
- Market posts end with "Not advice. Stop loss on." or "My opinion, not advice."
- Heading density: 1 heading per 250-350 words. A 1,400-word note is 4-5 H2s, not 15-19.
  Guides can run denser (6-8 H2s for 1,800-2,500 words) since readers scan for a sub-question.

## Why
The short-sentence, no-em-dash register is the biggest differentiator over competing prop-firm
affiliate blogs, and it's what makes the writing readable to an audience of mostly non-native
English speakers. Do not "fix" it toward more conventional, longer-sentence prose.

## Checklist before publishing any page
- [ ] grep the copy for `—` and `-` used as a connector (not hyphenated compound words)
- [ ] Count headings vs word count, check against density target
- [ ] Read the numbers out loud, check each one has a stated consequence
- [ ] Confirm disclaimer / disclosure lines are present where required (see monetization rules
      in build-plan-v2.md §8.2 and the money-page template)
